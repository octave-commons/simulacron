## What a simulacron is here

A simulacron is not just a chatbot or NPC.
It is a layered entity that:
- survives
- respects constraints
- forms beliefs
- evaluates meaning
- expresses through a cast of presences and tools

## Honest status

This repo is still concept-first. It exists to prevent the layered-cognition part of `fork_tales` from dissolving back into UI fragments, agent prompts, and mythic shorthand.
